The FRIES Framework & Healthy Communication

Consent is an ongoing, mutual agreement between people. Whether in romance, friendships, or digital spaces, healthy consent follows the FRIES model:

F: Freely Given

Made without pressure, guilt-tripping, manipulation, or the influence of alcohol or drugs. Silence or hesitation is not a "yes."

R: Reversible

Anyone can change their mind at any time, even in the middle of an activity or after saying yes earlier.

I: Informed

You can only consent when you have the full picture, such as knowing who will see a photo or what boundaries exist around intimacy.

E: Enthusiastic

Look for active excitement and clear body language ("I'd love to!"), rather than reluctant compliance ("I guess...").

S: Specific

Saying "yes" to one thing, like coming inside for a movie or taking a photo together, does not automatically mean "yes" to anything else.

Practical Communication Habits

  • ✓ Read non-verbal cues: Pulling back, stiffening up, avoiding eye contact, or going quiet are signals to pause and check in gently.
  • ✓ Make "No" easy to say: Frame questions so the other person feels zero pressure (e.g., "No worries at all if you're tired!").
  • ✓ Receive boundaries with grace: Thanking someone for sharing their boundary builds deep trust and emotional safety.
